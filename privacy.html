Privacy policy

hubert is a private tool used only by its owner. When the owner connects their Google account, hubert uses Google Drive access solely to read, organize and upload the owner's own files at the owner's request.

Data obtained through Google APIs is stored only on the owner's private server, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. hubert's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
